Privacy Policy

AI Family Locator (“Family Locator: Kids Tracker” on the App Store)
Effective date: 27 September 2026

This policy explains what data the AI Family Locator iOS app (“the App”) collects, why, who it is shared with and what choices you have. The App is operated by YeahApps Limited (“we”, “us”). Contact: y@yeahapps.me.

1. The short version

  • The App shares your location only with the people you invite to your family circle and who accept the invitation.
  • We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time in Profile → Delete account.

2. Data we collect and why

DataWhy we use it
Precise location and location history (including in the background, if you allow “Always”)To show you on your family’s map, build location history, detect arrivals and departures at places you set, send SOS alerts with your position and build driving reports.
Driving and movement data derived on your device from location and motion sensors: trips, speed, sudden braking or accelerationDriving reports and speed alerts for your family.
Battery level of your phoneShown to your family members and used for low-battery alerts.
Name, profile photoTo show who you are on the family map. Photos you choose as an avatar are uploaded to our image storage.
Phone number, email addressTo sign in, restore your account and invite family members. If you pick a person from your Contacts, only the phone number you select is used for the invitation; your address book is not uploaded.
Messages to the Family AI assistantTo generate answers. Your recent conversation is sent to our server and processed by an AI model provider acting on our behalf. The chat history is stored on your device.
Photo metadata (Journey feature, optional)The App reads only the capture time and geotag of photos on your device to draw your path. Photos never leave your device. If you choose to share your Journey with family, only visits (coordinates, start and end time, number of photos) are uploaded.
Identifiers: account ID, device installation ID, push notification tokenTo run your account, deliver notifications and keep the service secure.
PurchasesTo unlock Premium. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your card details.
Usage and diagnostic data: app interactions, crash reportsTo understand which features are used and to fix crashes. This data is not linked to your name.
IP addressTo determine your country for regional settings (e.g. the map provider).

3. Who can see your data

  • Your family circle: people you invited and who accepted can see your location, history, places, battery level, driving reports and SOS alerts. Joining always requires the member to install the App and accept the invitation on their own device.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf under contract: cloud hosting of our backend, Google Firebase (push notifications, analytics, crash reporting, remote configuration), Cloudinary (profile photo storage), an AI model provider (Family AI assistant) and IP geolocation. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, only when required by law.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

4. How long we keep data

Location history is kept while your account is active so your family can see it; account data is kept until you delete your account. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), your profile, places and location history are deleted from our servers within 30 days, except where we must keep limited records by law. Crash and analytics data is kept by Firebase according to its retention settings.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Change location, motion, contacts, photos and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Without location access the App cannot share your position.
  • Delete your account and data in the App: Profile → Delete account.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may request access to, correction of, export of or deletion of your personal data, and object to or restrict processing. Write to y@yeahapps.me; we answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Children

Families use the App to see where children are, so a child may be a member of a family circle. A child joins only on an invitation accepted on the child’s own device, and a parent or guardian should set up and supervise the child’s participation. We do not knowingly collect data from children for any purpose other than providing the family location service, and we never use it for advertising. If you believe a child joined without a parent’s consent, contact us and we will delete the data.

7. Security

Data exchanged with our servers is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access controls. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you of a breach where required by law.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the App.
