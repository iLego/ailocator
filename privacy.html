Who we are. The AI Family Locator mobile application (“the App”) is developed and operated by YeahApps Limited (“we”, “us”, “our”), which is also the data controller responsible for your personal data. Contact: y@yeahapps.me.

What this policy covers. This policy explains what personal and sensitive data the App collects on both the iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play) versions, why we collect it, how it is used, who it is shared with, how long we keep it and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the App and to our related websites at this domain.

1. The short version

  • We share your location only with the family members you invite to your circle and who accept your invitation on their own device.
  • We do not sell your personal data, do not use it for advertising and do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs and we do not request App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track you.
  • Optional Screen Time information on iOS is processed locally using Apple’s Family Controls and Device Activity frameworks. The App displays your own device’s aggregate screen-time duration; we do not upload or store raw app, website or Screen Time activity data on our servers.
  • Optional Family Wellness reads only step count and sleep data after you grant Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android) access. Raw health records stay on your device. If you separately enable “Share wellness with family”, only today’s step total, sleep duration and non-medical sleep-quality score are sent to our server and shown to accepted family members.
  • We collect the data needed to run the family location service, nothing more.
  • You can delete your account and data at any time — in the App (Profile → Delete account) or by writing to y@yeahapps.me from the web, even if you have uninstalled the App.
  • Data sent to and from our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

2a. Optional Family Wellness: Apple Health and Health Connect

Family Wellness is optional and read-only. On iOS we request read access to step count and sleep analysis through Apple Health / HealthKit. On Android we request read access to steps and sleep through Health Connect. You may refuse either permission. Location sharing and all unrelated features continue to work without health access.

The App reads the current day’s step total and the recent overnight sleep interval needed to prepare today’s summary. Sleep stages and recorded awake intervals may be used locally to calculate duration and continuity. We calculate a sleep-quality percentage on the device from sleep duration and recorded interruptions. It is an everyday wellness estimate only, not a medical measurement, diagnosis, treatment recommendation or emergency alert. Missing readings are not fabricated.

Raw records stay on your device. We do not upload individual HealthKit or Health Connect samples, sleep stages, timestamps for individual steps, or any other raw health records. We do not send health records or wellness summaries to analytics, crash-reporting services, advertising services or AI providers, and we never sell or use them for advertising, marketing, profiling, research or model training.

Family sharing requires a second, separate choice. Granting Apple Health or Health Connect permission does not share anything with your family. Only after you turn on “Share wellness with family” does the App send our server one privacy-minimized daily summary: the date, total steps, total sleep minutes and the non-medical sleep-quality score, plus the source platform (iOS or Android). Only authenticated family members you have connected with can request that summary.

Retention and deletion: our server keeps only your latest daily wellness summary; publishing a new day removes the previous stored summary. Turning family wellness sharing off deletes your stored wellness summary from our server. Account deletion also deletes it. The original Apple Health or Health Connect records remain controlled by you and the operating-system provider and are never modified by Family Locator.

Your choices: you can leave family sharing off and still view your own available wellness data locally. You can turn family sharing off at any time in the App. You can also revoke Apple Health access in iOS Health settings or revoke Health Connect access in Android settings / Health Connect. Revoking system access stops future reads; it does not delete your original health records.

3. Device permissions we request

  • Location (while using and background “Always”) — to share your position with your circle and power alerts. Background location is optional.
  • Motion & Fitness — to detect driving and movement for driving reports.
  • Contacts — only to let you pick a phone number to invite. Your full address book is not uploaded.
  • Photos — only for the optional Journey feature, to read capture time and geotags on your device; photos are not uploaded.
  • Notifications — for arrival/departure, SOS, low-battery and account alerts.
  • Apple Health / HealthKit (iOS, optional) — read-only access to step count and sleep analysis for Family Wellness. Raw health records stay on your device. A daily aggregate leaves the device only if you separately enable family wellness sharing, as detailed in section 2a.
  • Health Connect (Android, optional) — read-only access to steps and sleep for Family Wellness. Raw Health Connect records stay on your device. A daily aggregate leaves the device only if you separately enable family wellness sharing, as detailed in section 2a.
  • Screen Time / Family Controls (iOS only, optional) — only when you tap “Enable Screen Time”. Apple presents the system authorization UI. The Screen Time report is rendered on-device by Apple’s Device Activity framework; denying this access does not block the rest of the App.

You can change or withdraw any permission at any time in iOS Settings → AI Family Locator or Android Settings → Apps → AI Family Locator → Permissions. Denying location access prevents us from sharing your position with your family, but the App remains usable to a limited extent.

  • Provide the Family AI assistant when you choose to use it.
  • Process purchases and restore entitlements.
  • Detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • Diagnose crashes and improve the App.

We do not use your personal data to serve advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

6. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above:

  • Location history — kept while your account is active so your family can see it, and you can delete locations individually where the App allows.
  • Account data, profile, places and location history — deleted when you delete your account, as described in section 7, and otherwise within 30 days of a deletion request.
  • Purchase records — kept as long as required for tax, accounting and legal purposes.
  • Crash and analytics data — kept by Firebase according to its retention settings, and generally for no longer than 14 months.
  • Security and abuse logs — kept for up to 12 months.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and associated data at any time:

  • In the App: Profile → Delete account.
  • On the web (no App needed): send an email from any address to y@yeahapps.me with the subject “Delete my account” and the phone number or email associated with your account. We will verify the request and complete the deletion.

When you delete your account we delete your profile, places, location history and associated personal data from our servers within 30 days, except where we must retain limited records to comply with law (for example purchase/tax records). Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription — you must cancel the subscription separately in the relevant store.

No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a personal data breach where required by law.

10. Children’s privacy

Families use the App to see where their children are, so a child may be a member of a family circle. We take children’s privacy seriously:

  • A child joins only through an invitation that a parent or legal guardian creates and that is accepted on the child’s own device. A parent or guardian should set up and supervise the child’s participation and should be the account holder.
  • We collect a child’s data only to provide the family location service. We never use children’s data for advertising or behavioural targeting and we do not build profiles of children for marketing.
  • For the Google Play version, we comply with the Google Play Families Policy and the Families Self-Certified Ads SDK requirements; our App contains no advertising SDKs. For the App Store version we comply with applicable children’s privacy rules (COPPA and equivalent laws).
  • We do not knowingly collect more data from children than is necessary for the service. If you believe a child has used the App without a parent’s or guardian’s consent, contact us at y@yeahapps.me and we will delete the data and account.
